## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-trust, conversion-engineered aesthetic tailored for senior-level growth, performance marketing, and product leadership portfolios. Rooted in refined minimalism, it strips away decorative bloat in favor of surgical typography, structured content containers, and intentional micro-accents.

The atmosphere is authoritative, precise, and crisp. It eschews heavy black fills and over-saturated color blocks, favoring warm off-white canvases, hairline borders, and targeted soft red/coral focal points that drive conversion actions without inducing visual fatigue. Typography is kept strictly within light and medium weights to preserve editorial elegance and airy readability.

## Colors

The palette balances clean slate neutrals with a strategic crimson/coral accent system.

- **Primary (`#E11D48`)**: Reserved exclusively for primary conversion elements, active pill tabs, key metric highlights, and directional badges.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep slate for high-contrast, razor-sharp typography and anchor iconography. Avoid pure black to maintain a high-end editorial feel.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC`)**: Base tone for structural backgrounds, subtle card insets, and alternating section strips.
- **Accents & Tints**: Soft coral surfaces (`#FFF1F2`) paired with refined red text (`#E11D48`) define status tags, highlight pills, and urgency micro-indicators.

## Typography

The typography uses a unified family throughout, emphasizing a clean, contemporary rhythm. Thick, heavy weights (700+) are avoided entirely to uphold a light, executive appearance. 

Headings rely on a maximum weight of 600 (Semi-Bold) with tight negative tracking to maintain focus and poise. Body text uses 400 (Regular) with generous line heights to ensure frictionless scanning across multi-column module grids and case breakdowns. Labels and badges use 500 (Medium) with subtle letter spacing for crisp clarity at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of 1120px for portfolio content and 768px for deep-dive case studies. 

- **Vertical Rhythm**: Large section gaps (`4rem` to `6rem`) establish clear breaks between proofs, modules, and offer blocks.
- **Card Padding**: Structured content containers use balanced internal padding (`1.25rem` to `1.75rem`) to maintain an airy aesthetic while remaining modular.
- **Micro-Gaps**: Metric lists and credential pills rely on compact spacing (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`) to keep vital conversion data grouped and easily glanceable.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on low-contrast hairline borders and soft ambient diffusion rather than standard drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#FAFAFA` base background.
- **Level 1 (Card/Container)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` fill framed with a 1px border (`#F1F5F9` or `#E2E8F0`). Flat surface with no shadow.
- **Level 2 (Hover/Floating)**: An ultra-diffuse ambient shadow (`0 8px 24px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`) combined with a border transition to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modal/Overlays)**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with a deep blur shadow (`0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) and high backdrop-filter blur (`12px`) on the surrounding scrim.

## Shapes

The design balances structured cards with rounded pill accents.

- **Cards & Modules**: Follow a standard 12px (`0.75rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) border radius, offering modern structure without looking playful.
- **CTAs, Badges & Pills**: Feature fully rounded, pill-shaped edges (`9999px`) to create high tactile contrast against the rectangular content grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), solid `#E11D48` background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-medium`, with balanced horizontal padding (`px-6 py-3`). On hover, shifts smoothly to `#BE123C`.
- **Secondary CTA**: Pill-shaped with a pure `#FFFFFF` background, a 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and `#0F172A` text. Hover states shift subtly to a `#F8FAFC` background.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Feature Badges**: Rounded pill (`rounded-full`), micro-typography (`11px` or `12px`, `font-medium`). Styled with a soft coral background (`#FFF1F2`), 1px border (`#FECDD3`), and crisp coral text (`#E11D48`).
- **Category Insets**: Neutral slate pill (`#F1F5F9`) with secondary text (`#64748B`) for subtle classification.

### Content Cards
- **Module & Deliverable Grids**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background encased in a 1px `#F1F5F9` border. Inner content features an understated header badge, a medium-weight headline, and secondary slate body copy.
- **Proof & Metric Cards**: Centered or left-aligned key figures using medium weights, accompanied by label-sm descriptors and faint divider rules.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Flat `#FFFFFF` fields with a hairline `#E2E8F0` border, soft inset padding (`px-4 py-3`), and subtle `#94A3B8` placeholder text. Focus triggers an outline ring in coral (`#FECDD3`) and a crisp `#E11D48` border.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Minimalist 16px squares/circles with `#E2E8F0` borders that fill with `#E11D48` upon selection.
